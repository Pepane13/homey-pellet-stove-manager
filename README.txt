Pellet Stove Manager monitors and helps you manage a pellet stove fitted with a Duepi EVO board (Interstoves, myDPRemote Wi-Fi module and other brands using the same board).

The app connects to the stove through the myDPRemote server and shows its status, alarms, flue gas and board temperatures, auger and exhaust fan speeds, power level and hour counters (total and since the last service). Flow cards let you react to alarms, status changes and loss of connection.

Choose any temperature sensor of your Homey for each role: comfort room, thermostat, outdoor reference and backup. Every reading is checked, and the bias of the backup outdoor sensor is learnt. The outdoor forecast can come from a Homey weather device or from Météo-France (AROME model).

The app learns how your house loses and gains heat, computes when the stove must start to reach a target temperature at a given time ("Reach 21 °C at 06:20") and raises a Flow trigger at that moment. A nightly heat-loss curve lets you measure the effect of insulation works.

The app only reads the stove: it does not send any command to it.
